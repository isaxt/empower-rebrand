01Context and problem

Empower already has a story.

Empower is a rideshare network where drivers set their own rates and keep 100% of the fare, and riders choose drivers they trust. The mission was real. The brand and the app were not yet saying so.

30M+Rides
750K+Riders
$500+Driver earnings

Figures as published on Empower’s own site.

Before · the website tells the story

What’s working

  • The mission is authentic
  • Real testimonials and data back it up
  • Zero commission, driver-set rates, rider preferences
  • Drivers and riders can already articulate the value in their own words

What’s missing

  • Messaging doesn’t have a single throughline
  • Visual identity doesn’t match the ambition of the story it’s telling
  • App UI doesn’t visibly deliver on the promises the website makes

The promise lived in the copy. The product never showed it.

02Concept and strategy

You have a say.

Every screen, every price, every match.

  1. The driver has a voice
  2. The rider has a choice
  3. The platform makes the connection
Mission
Gives drivers real pricing power and gives riders choice. Both parties have autonomy and choice.
Brand promise
You have a say. Every screen, every price, every match.
Unique positioning
Not trying to be a “cheaper Uber” or the “ethical Lyft”. It is its own category: mobility built on mutual control and agreement.

04Key design explorations

Three decisions that carry the idea.

Decision 01

Show the drivers, then the price.

The old flow asked for a destination and returned a car. The redesign opens on the people: trusted drivers nearby, favorites marked, and a ride list that pairs each driver with their own fare and a local range for the trip.

Decision 02

Same functions, one voice.

Account, help and credits already existed. The work was making them sound and look like the brand: serif headlines for orientation, quiet Inter for utility, one blue instead of three.

Decision 03

The driver sets the rate.

On the driver side the rate is a first-class screen, with a suggested rate and nearby drivers for context. Earnings close the loop and end on a single number: 100% you keep.

06Outcome and reflection

One idea, from billboard to button.

The result is a brand that says the same thing everywhere it appears: on a windshield, on a wall, and on the screen in a rider’s hand. The campaign lines and the interface make the same promise, and the interface now backs it up.

What was delivered

  • A strategy: mission, promise and positioning
  • An identity: mark, wordmark, color, type
  • A campaign across billboards and vehicles
  • 15 primary screens across the rider and driver apps
